About the park

A sheltered 6 acres, 15 minutes from the city

Alpine View Holiday Park & Motels is located on a sheltered 6 acres with tree-lined boundaries, just 15 minutes from Christchurch City. You’ll find us at 650 Main South Rd in Templeton.

The complex brings together a motel, a holiday park and a backpackers lodge, so whether you’d like a studio unit, a powered or non-powered site, or a bed in the lodge, it’s all on the one property.

  • Motel10 new 1 and 2-bedroom studio units
  • Holiday Park85 powered and 30 non-powered tent sites
  • Backpackers Lodge20 beds, with group rates available

Good to know

Questions guests often ask

Where is Alpine View Holiday Park?

We’re at 650 Main South Rd, Templeton, Christchurch – on a sheltered 6 acres with tree-lined boundaries, just 15 minutes from Christchurch City.

What kind of accommodation do you have?

The complex includes a Motel with 10 studio units, a Holiday Park with powered and non-powered tent sites, and a 20-bed Backpackers Lodge.

How much does it cost to stay?

Motel Units are $130 – $200, Holiday Park sites are $25 – $35, and the Backpacker Lodge is from $30. Please phone or email for rates on your dates.

Do you have powered sites?

Yes. The Holiday Park has 85 powered and 30 non-powered tent sites of varying sizes.

What beds are in the motel units?

Our 10 new ground and first-floor 1 and 2-bedroom studio units have super-king and king-size single beds.

Can you accommodate groups?

Yes – the 20-bed backpacker lodge is great for groups, and group rates are available. Get in touch to talk through your group’s needs.
